# Core Gameplay — Ability Inspirations

Date: 2026-08-03
Status: Exploration notes, not an approved loadout

## Current gameplay direction

The player defends Montréal from attacking enemies. Civilian survival determines the quality of the victory, while the mission is lost only if the player's mech or a critical city target is destroyed. This is the **Hybrid protection rule**.

Current weapon and ability ideas:

- A shield that catches incoming projectiles and throws them back.
- Rapid-fire Gatling-style cannons.
- A powerful precision gun or heavy shot.
- Rockets limited by a cooldown or charge.
- A meter that builds during combat. When activated, bullets temporarily become smart and no longer require precise manual targeting.

## Emerging ability structure

A focused first loadout could contain:

1. **Primary:** Gatling cannons for sustained enemy clearing.
2. **Defensive hand:** projectile-catching shield with aimed return throw.
3. **Heavy attack:** precision laser/heavy shot using energy or a short cooldown.
4. **Ordnance:** rocket burst on a longer cooldown.
5. **Core state:** Smart Ammo, earned through effective combat and defense.

VTOL Hover and Tripwire should remain in the idea bank until the basic combat loop proves it needs more positioning or area-control tools. Not all referenced abilities need to appear in the game.

## Open design questions

- Does the energy meter reward damage and kills only, or also reward interceptions and saved civilians?
- Are Smart Ammo and the missile barrage two selectable core abilities, or are rockets a normal cooldown ability?
- Can the shield return every projectile, or do some attacks need dodging, cover, or destruction?
- Can Smart Ammo accidentally target enemies near civilians or fragile buildings, and how does the player control that risk?
- Does vertical movement improve city defense enough to justify VR comfort and production costs?
